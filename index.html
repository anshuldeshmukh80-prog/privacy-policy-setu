Setu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Setu ("the app") links your TV, phone and Windows laptop on your own Wi-Fi network so you can use one as a remote for another, send files, share the clipboard and show screens. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Setu does not collect, store or send your personal information to the developer or to any server on the internet. Everything Setu does happens directly between your own devices on your local network (and, if you choose, over Bluetooth). There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking in the app.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Local network and Wi-Fi: to find your other devices and to send files, clipboard text and screen pictures directly between them. Data goes only to the devices you choose and allow.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): an optional way to link to a TV. It is used only to pair and to carry small messages such as clipboard text.
  • Files you choose: the app only reads files that you pick to send, using Android's file picker. Files other devices send to you are saved to your Downloads folder (phone) or to the app's own Files folder (TV).
  • Clipboard: the app reads or writes the clipboard only when you tap a button, share to it, or turn on clipboard sync while the app is open.
  • Screen capture: when you tap "Start showing my screen", Android asks for your permission and then the phone's screen is sent to the device you picked. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Audio of your phone (optional): if you choose to play your phone's sound on the TV, the app copies what your phone is playing (not the microphone) and sends it to the TV while you share. Android asks for permission first.
  • Notifications: to show that screen sharing is active.
  • TV remote: the app talks to the Android TV Remote Service built into your TV, on your local network, to press remote buttons. A pairing code you type is used only for that.

Data sharing and retention

The developer receives no data from the app. Setu does not sell, rent or share personal information. Files and clipboard text pass between your devices and are not stored by the developer. Device names and security tokens that let your devices recognise each other are stored only on your own devices, and you can remove them with "Forget devices".

Children

Setu is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here with a new date.
